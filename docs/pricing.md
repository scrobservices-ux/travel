All packages: fixed price, fixed scope, delivered in 72 hours, one month of support included. 50% on order, 50% on delivery. Payable by MTN MoMo, Orange Money, or bank transfer.

## Bundles

- **Bot + Booking** : 350 000 setup + 50 000/mois (salons, cliniques)
- **Boutique complète** (Storefront + AI Assistant) : 650 000 setup + 100 000/mois
- Second product for an existing client: −20% on setup.

## Rules that keep this profitable

1. **Never custom-quote.** If a prospect's need doesn't fit a package, it's either a retainer change request (small) or not our client (large). The packages are the business.
2. **Scope changes go on the retainer.** "Can it also do X?" → "Yes — that's included in your monthly plan" (if small) or "that's the upgrade to package Y" (if big).
3. **The retainer is the business.** 20 clients × ~45 000 average = 900 000 FCFA/month recurring before any new sale. Churn protection: respond to change requests within 24h.
4. **Annual prepay:** 10 months for the price of 12 paid upfront (cash flow > margin early on).

## Unit economics sanity check (AI Assistant)

- Retainer 75 000 FCFA ≈ $120/month.
- Hosting share on a shared VPS: ~$2/client. Claude API at SMB volume: typically $10–30.
- Margin stays >60% even on the busiest clients; cap exposure by the 1,024-token reply limit and 20-turn history already in the template.
